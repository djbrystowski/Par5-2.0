How to play

Build a six-man team from real PGA Tour seasons. Elite picks score a Hole in One.

  1. Spin a random season, 2001–2026.
  2. Pick one golfer's season from that year's players.
  3. Slot him into one of six spots: Wins, Top 5s, Top 10s, Cut %, Under Par or Money. That spot scores his number in that stat. Cut % needs at least 15 events, and counts more the more events a player played.
  4. Repeat six times. Every spot gets filled once. You get 2 mulligans (↻) per team to redraw a season.
Money is a player's share of that season's total prize pool, so 2004 and 2024 count the same. A major counts as 1.5 wins. The catch: you can't see future spins. Burn a monster season on the wrong spot and you'll regret it.

About the data

Every official PGA Tour event from 2001 through the 2026 Mexico Open, from ESPN, with winners of the events ESPN lacks (WGC Match Play, The International, Barracuda, some Zurich team years) added from Wikipedia. Money is a player's official earnings as a share of the season's total prize pool (actual dollars in parentheses). In the Wins slot, a major counts as 1.5 wins. Cut % needs 15+ events and is weighted by events played (15 events = 60% credit, 27+ = full). Under Par counts both the season total and strokes under par per round, so doing it in fewer events counts more. Under Par is a player's total strokes to par for the season. Seasons follow the PGA Tour's calendar, including wraparound years. 2026 is through November 1.
