How to play

Goal: build the best 5-round card you can. Hit 99% of a perfect card for the Tiger Slam.

  1. Spin for a random year, tournament and round.
  2. Pick one player's round. You only see the scorecard. Finish and prize money are hidden.
  3. Lock in to reveal your points and the best pick you could have made.
  4. Repeat 5 times. You get 2 respins (↻) per card, on any reel.
Points come from beating the field, birdies, eagles, bogey-free rounds, and how the player finished that week. Winning is worth a lot, so the low round isn't always the best pick.
